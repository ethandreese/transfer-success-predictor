⚽ Transfer Success Predictor

Predicts how a football transfer is likely to go, trained on ~5,000 real permanent transfers (2013–2026) using pre-transfer performance, age, fee, and destination club strength. Success score blends performance level and change, market-value growth, playing time, and value for money — measured over the player's entire tenure at the new club, not just year one. Loan spells are scored separately (see Browse loans) since they aren't a permanent-transfer decision.
