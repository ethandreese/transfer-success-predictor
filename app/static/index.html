About

A football transfer analytics site built on ~8,300 real scored permanent transfers and ~4,300 loan spells (2013–2026). Predict how a hypothetical move might go, browse every real scored transfer, and dig into surprises, club recruitment records, player careers, and league-wide fee trends, all built from the same historical scores.

Start here

Search a real player and a destination club to see a predicted success score, or jump straight into the real historical data: every scored transfer, loan, club, player career, and league is browsable.
