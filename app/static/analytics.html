About

Every scored transfer, plotted a few different ways.

Five charts drawn from every scored permanent transfer (~8,300 of them). On the scatter charts, hover a point for a quick look or click/tap it for that transfer's full breakdown card. Fee, market value, and height are each missing for a small number of transfers (a free/undisclosed fee, no market-value snapshot at the time, or no recorded height), so those charts plot only the transfers that have the number they need.
