About

Search a real player and a destination club to see how a hypothetical transfer might score. Trained on ~7,800 real permanent transfers (2013–2026), the model only ever sees information available before a move happens: age, recent form, fee, and the two clubs involved, the same constraint a real prediction has to live with. Loan moves are judged differently and scored on their own formula (see Browse loans); see About for what the 0–100 score itself measures.
