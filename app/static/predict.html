⚽ Transfer Success Predictor

Predicts how a football transfer is likely to go, trained on ~7,800 real permanent transfers (2013–2026, nearly the full scored dataset - a small remainder lacks a required pre-transfer stat, e.g. an undisclosed fee) using pre-transfer performance, age, fee, and destination club strength. Success score blends performance level and change, market-value growth, playing time, and value for money, measured over the player's entire tenure at the new club, not just year one. Loan spells are scored separately (see Browse loans) since they aren't a permanent-transfer decision.
