About

Browse scored loan spells, graded the same way as a permanent transfer, minus the two components that don't apply to a loan (value for money and resale profit, since most loans carry no real fee and don't end in a sale of their own). Playing time counts for more here: whether the loan actually delivered game time is usually the central question it gets judged on.
