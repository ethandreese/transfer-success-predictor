About

One row per club that bought or sold at least one scored permanent transfer, built entirely from the same historical scores shown on Browse. In/Avg score covers players this club bought; Resale covers the subset it later sold on again (profit or loss vs. what it paid); Out/Departures covers how players this club sold away went on to perform at their next club. A club needs at least 5 transfers (3 for resales) before it's eligible for a rate-based ranking - a club with one lucky signing isn't "the best recruiter in Europe."
