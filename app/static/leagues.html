About

Every league with at least 15 scored permanent transfers, comparing its earliest 3 complete years against its most recent 3 complete years (the current, still-in-progress year is excluded from every average - it runs far below a normal season's transfer count). A league needs at least 6 complete years of history before a trend can be shown at all; any single year shown in the chart still needs at least 5 transfers of its own to avoid a noisy one-transfer average.
